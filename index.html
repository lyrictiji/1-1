01 The Areté Standard

A quiet, repeatable process — not a personality to copy.

Every 1-on-1 week runs through the same four checkpoints, so progress is something we can actually measure together instead of guess at.

01Prep

Build your map

Before the bell, we write down the levels, the bias, and the one setup that invalidates the day — together, on a call or in your channel.

02Execute

Trade the plan

Entries, stops, and size are decided before the candle prints. We work on removing mid-trade improvising, one session at a time.

03Journal

Log it, honestly

Every session goes in your private journal. I read it. That accountability is the actual product — not another PDF.

04Review

Weekly 1-on-1

We sit down weekly, screen-share your charts and journal, and fix the one habit that's actually costing you money.

05 FAQ

Questions before you apply?

Do I need experience before joining?

+

No — but you should be comfortable on a sim or small size. Replace this with your real onboarding requirement.

What's the difference between Student and Premium?

+

Student covers the vault, journaling, and group calls. Premium adds the weekly private 1-on-1 and priority booking. Edit the pricing cards above to match your real offer.

How does Discord role access work?

+

After you apply and your role is granted in Discord, you'd sign in here with Discord so the site can check your role. See the note in the Member Dashboard section — that live check needs a real backend to be connected.

Can I see my own journal history?

+

Yes — every entry you log is private to you and the mentor. Only the mentor (owner) can see every member's journal; members only ever see their own.

Do you guarantee results?

+

No. Trading carries real risk. What's guaranteed is the structure — a real weekly call, a mentor reading your journal, and a process you can repeat.
