02 Psychology

Trading is a mental game — we train for that too.

Strategy only gets you so far. Most losses come from the moment after the plan breaks — revenge trades, moved stops, oversized positions. Psychology work is built into the mentorship, not sold as a separate add-on.

01Identify

Spot your patterns

We find the specific habits that are actually costing you — revenge trading, moving stops, oversizing after a loss — by reading it straight out of your journal.

02Reframe

Change the story

Most self-sabotage comes from a belief, not a lack of skill. We work through what's actually driving the impulsive decision in the moment.

03Build

Daily discipline habits

Simple routines and checklists — reviewed in your daily market recap — that keep emotion out of execution before it costs you money.

04Support

Ongoing accountability

Regular check-ins so a slip back into old habits gets caught in days, not months.

05 FAQ

Questions before you apply?

Do I need experience before joining?

+

No — but you should be comfortable on a sim or small size. Replace this with your real onboarding requirement.

What's the difference between Student and 1-on-1 Mentorship?

+

Student covers the full lecture vault, group calls, live trading sessions, and your own journal. 1-on-1 Mentorship adds a private weekly call, weekly journal reviews, and direct homework review — Student members are never prioritized over 1-on-1 members for calls or reviews.

Do you guarantee results?

+

No. Trading carries real risk. What's guaranteed is the structure — a real weekly call, a mentor reading your journal, and a process you can repeat.
